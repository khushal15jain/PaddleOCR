DAVID, Jean
Suddenly at home on Wednesday, November 28, 2001 at the age of 88. Beloved wife of the late Alfred David. Loving daughter of the late Oscar and Lizzie Zeitlin. Dear sister of David (Duddy) of California. Predeceased by her brothers Mortimer (Mucky), Jack and Solly and her sisters Sophie, Mary and Helen. Aunt Jean was much loved and respected and will truly be missed by her many nieces and nephews. Heartfelt thanks to Dr. David Kalin for his many years of kindness and understanding, to the wonderful staff of Riverpark Place, and to caregivers Elizabeth and Antoinette for the special care and warmth they provided. A Graveside Service will be held on Sunday, December 2, 2001 at 2 p.m. in the Chapel at the New Jewish Community Cemetery, Osgoode, ON. Those wishing may make donations to the charity of their choice. Arrangements in care of the Central Chapel of Hulse, Playfair & McGarry.
